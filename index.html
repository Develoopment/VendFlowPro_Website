Common questions from hosts

What property managers usually want to know before saying yes. Something else on your mind? Call or text (469) 465 5915.

What will this cost me?

It depends on your location and the setup that suits it, so we go through the options with you at your free site visit. Restocking is always free.

How much room and power does it need?

The cabinet measures 28.6″ W × 31.2″ D × 80.6″ H. Leave about 31″ of wall width for clearance; it fits through a standard 34″ door. Two machines side by side need roughly 57″, three need about 86″.

It runs on a regular grounded 120V outlet with no special wiring or plumbing, and connects over Wi-Fi or its own cellular connection.

What temperatures can it run at, and how much fits?

As a refrigerator it stays between 33 and 44 °F, with temperature monitored around the clock. The same cabinet can instead run as a freezer (−10 to 0 °F) or as dry shelving for snacks. It holds up to 336 twenty-ounce bottles.

Does every product need its own slot?

No. The cameras read the whole shelf rather than fixed lanes, so products can be arranged any way that suits your building and rearranged whenever tastes change.

Which payment methods work?

It's fully cashless: tap-to-pay, Apple Pay, Google Wallet, and chip or swipe cards, with digital receipts. The specific card brands accepted depend on the payment reader installed; some readers don't take Amex, Discover or prepaid cards, and we'll tell you which applies to your machine.

What if a customer is charged incorrectly?

They can report it at the end of their purchase. We review the transaction, charge the correct amount and send an updated receipt. Your staff never has to get involved.

How often is it restocked?

Usually every one to two weeks, adjusted to how fast your machine sells. Problems and temperature issues reach us automatically, and we respond to service calls the same day.

How quickly can it be installed?

Typically within 7–10 days of approval. Once it's plugged in at its final spot, setup and system checks take another 24–48 hours before it starts selling.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. You sign a short placement agreement that covers access and liability, and that's it; it's not a lease or a purchase. High-traffic locations may qualify for a commission, and we'll be upfront about whether yours does.

Is my location a good fit?

Usually, if around 25 or more people pass through each day. Offices, warehouses, gyms, schools, apartment buildings and clinics all work well. Send us your address and we'll confirm that it's in our service area.
